About ✦

I'm only at
the beginning.

I've wanted to make music for as long as I can remember. It has always been the dream — turning feelings, memories and the things I can't always explain into something you can hear. “Wanna Know” is my first release, but to me it feels like the first page of something much bigger. I'm still becoming the artist I know I can be, and I want the people who find me now to be here for everything that comes next.

— Jay Noir
